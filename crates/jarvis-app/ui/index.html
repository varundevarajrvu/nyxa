How to use Nyxa

Wake me by clapping three times (or saying “Hey Jarvis” for now) — wait for the core to light up, then say what you want.

A “Hey Nyxa” voice wake is coming soon; for now, clapping is your quickest way in.

Open apps & websites

“open gmail”
“open spotify”
“open youtube and search for lo-fi beats”

Play music & videos

“play blinding lights on spotify”
“play a workout playlist on youtube”
“pause the music” · “next track” · “volume up”

Write an email

“write an email to john saying I’ll be late”

Opens a ready-to-review draft — it never sends on its own.

Close & tidy up asks first

“close spotify”
“close the youtube tab”
“delete report.pdf”

These ask before acting. For close/move Nyxa says “yes or no?” — just answer out loud. For delete it pops a window you must click.

Move it with your hands camera

Click Gesture (top-right) to turn on hand control. A small camera preview appears bottom-left of the network.

  • One hand — move it around and the network follows
  • Two hands — spread apart to zoom in, bring together to zoom out

All on-device — nothing leaves your laptop. Turn it back off to save battery.

Reading the network

Each dot is something Nyxa can do, grouped by area. Dots grow the more you use them. Hover one for details, click it to filter the log. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom.

  • Green — runs instantly
  • Gold — asks “yes or no?” first
  • Rose — needs an on-screen click

Reading the core

  • Slow glow — waiting for 3 claps or “Hey Jarvis”
  • Bright, with rings — listening to your command
  • Orbiting arcs — thinking
  • Gold ring — waiting for your yes / no
  • A signal runs out — doing that action
  • Grey & still — paused

Stopping Nyxa

Wake me and say “stop”, press Ctrl + Shift + Alt + K, or click Pause. It stays in the tray — reopen it from the tray icon.
